Three layers: main trace `public/scenes/faq-trace.svg` (~620 KB); finer trace over wall and floor `faq-soft.svg` (~1.5 MB, traced with shadows lifted, TRACE_GAMMA=0.5, so dark steps are not merged); contrast-stretched trace of the lamp halo (wall + panel) `faq-glow.svg` (~275 KB, feathered edges). Night call: `TRACE_GAMMA=0.5 python3 -I trace-scene.py <ref> <out> faq soft.json faces.json`. Reusable for Contact.
