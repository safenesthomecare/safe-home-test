# CF-36: Figure rework Phases A and B (3D orientation and pose anatomy)
**Problem:** figures were one side profile mirrored by `face`; from behind the hair still drew on the back of the head, and torso, arms and legs did not change with viewing angle.
**Done:** `figure.jsx` rewritten as a small 3D skeleton projected to flat shapes. New `view` prop (front, f34, side default, b34, back) plus `look` (head turn). Head and hair are drawn per view: back views cover the whole back of the head (bun on top, long hair falls over the back, shorter to the shoulders); front views show fringe and side locks. Torso width, shoulder and hip width follow the yaw; cardigan front and scrub V-neck show only when facing the camera; back collar from behind. All 8 poses redefined in 3D (arm [forward, outward] angles; leg thigh/shin); pelvis height solved so the lower foot is planted; walk has opposite arm/leg swing, shoulder-hip counter-rotation, lifted and pitched trailing foot; sit uses hip-back and foreshortened thighs; elder gets forward lean, head ahead of shoulders and soft knees. Depth-sorted draw order. Camera pitch cue (nearer parts a little lower).
**Compatibility:** existing `pose`, `face`, `walk`, `dusk`, `elder`, `item`, `cane` unchanged; default `view` is `side`, so all six worlds still render. Worlds not edited yet (Phase C).
**Verified:** test sheets for both characters, 8 poses x 5 views, viewed; real CRA build compiles; element counts all under caps (Services 511 els / 52 gradients, was 577 / 58); Services world frame viewed.
**Not done:** Phase C (turn figures in worlds, e.g. PSW walking away or toward camera) and Phase D re-run of contrast audit and fps. Known: `glasses` prop removed (it drew nothing); cream trousers are low contrast on cream backgrounds (unchanged colours).
