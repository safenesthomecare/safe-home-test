# SHOTS.md: shot bible (Phase 0)
Coordinates are fractions of the frame (W wide, H tall), origin top-left. Flat solid colours only: no gradients, blur or soft shadows.

## Cast (Phase 1)
- PSW: blue scrubs `#0066B3` (hearth-blue), navy shoes `#003B66`. Patient: orange cardigan `#F47A1F` (warm-orange), cream trousers `#FFF9F2`. Faceless silhouettes (see FIGURE-STYLE.md).
- Dusk (Contact): scrubs shift to `#003B66`-side tone, cardigan to `#B85207` (ember) so the pair stays readable on dark.

## Rules for every scene
- Grid: pair on the vertical thirds (x 0.33 and 0.67). Patient eyes line at y 0.33. Look room ~W/3 on the side each faces.
- Positive space (about 65-75%): the story, the pair, the main set. Negative space (25-35%): empty area holding only dark foreground shapes in the outer third or bottom 12-18%. Nothing important in it.
- Three tones: light key shape, mid-tone set, dark foreground (15-25% brightness), each a separate solid shape.
- Depth: foreground 10-20% H (dark, 1.0-1.5x speed), midground 40-60% (pair, 0.5-0.7x), background 20-40% (0.1-0.3x).
- Text panel sits on the side opposite the dark foreground mass, over a calm mid-tone.
- Phone: pair drops to the lower third; top area left free for the panel.
- Camera: no rotation. Angles come only from y position and zoom.
